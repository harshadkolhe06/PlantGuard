# PlantGuard — Botanical Plant-Care Prototype 🌿

> **"Your Plants Deserve a Natural Touch."**

PlantGuard is an experimental, water-based plant-care spray developed as a student chemical prototype. It combines neem, garlic, Reetha, aloe vera, and glycerine to explore a botanical approach to keeping unwanted insects away from household plants.

---

## 📋 Project Highlights

- **Standard Batch Size:** 100 ml
- **Prototype Batch Pricing:** ₹65 per 100 ml bottle
- **Formulation Base:** Distilled water with botanical extracts and vegetable glycerine
- **Interactive Web App:** Includes an interactive spray demonstration, batch volume & valuation calculator, laboratory preparation guide, safety & SDS documentation, and SDG alignment.

---

## 🍃 Botanical Ingredients

1. **Neem Powder / Extract (`2.0 g / 100 ml`):** Primary natural botanical agent traditionally utilized for plant-care and pest management.
2. **Garlic Extract (`0.5 g / 100 ml`):** Pungent botanical extract rich in natural sulfur compounds.
3. **Reetha (Soapnut) Extract (`0.5 g / 100 ml`):** Source of natural saponins acting as a biodegradable surfactant and wetting agent.
4. **Aloe Vera Extract (`0.5 g / 100 ml`):** Plant-derived soothing agent supporting surface adherence.
5. **Vegetable Glycerine (`1.0 ml / 100 ml`):** Mild humectant minimizing rapid evaporation of water droplets on foliage.
6. **Distilled Water:** Carrier solvent bringing final formulation volume to 100 ml.

---

## 🌐 Web Application Features

- **Interactive Spray Simulation:** Realistic interactive garden animation showing plant foliage, pest departure, and botanical mist deposition.
- **Dynamic Batch & Price Calculator:** Real-time scaling of active raw materials with complete bottle yield calculations and estimated selling value at ₹65 / 100 ml.
- **Safety & SDS Compliance:** Comprehensive Material Safety Data Sheet links and laboratory handling precautions for each ingredient.
- **UN Sustainable Development Goals:** Direct alignment with **SDG 12** (*Responsible Consumption & Production*) and **SDG 15** (*Life on Land*).
- **Interactive Step-by-Step Preparation:** Visual walkthrough covering weighing, hot-water infusion, filtration, surfactant blending, and bottling.

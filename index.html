12 RESPONSIBLE CONSUMPTION & PRODUCTION
♻️

Targeting Sustainable Sourcing & Minimal Waste

The PlantGuard formulation investigates the replacement of synthetic chemical pesticides with renewable, plant-derived active ingredients (neem, garlic, Reetha, aloe vera). Measured small-batch formulation minimizes laboratory raw-material surplus, and water-based dispersion avoids petroleum-derived solvents.

  • Renewable Inputs: Prioritizing botanical extracts and natural saponins over synthetic compounds.
  • Container Reusability: Standardized 100 ml spray bottles designed for refilling and multi-cycle testing.
  • Waste Minimization: Documented batch scaling via the interactive calculator prevents material waste.
ℹ️ Academic prototype exploration; industrial lifecycle assessment (LCA) has not yet been conducted.
15 LIFE ON LAND
🌱

Protecting Soil Microbiomes & Terrestrial Foliage

Conventional domestic chemical pesticides frequently accumulate in potting soil, leaching into indoor drainage and harming soil biodiversity. PlantGuard explores whether mild, naturally biodegradable botanical compounds can repel unwanted insect pests without causing leaf phytotoxicity or lingering soil toxicity.

  • Terrestrial Compatibility: Mandatory patch testing protocols to observe individual plant leaf health.
  • Biodegradable Base: Water and food-grade vegetable glycerine dissolve and disperse naturally without persistent soil residues.
  • Pollinator Safety Research: Focused on non-systemic, mechanical and olfactory insect repellency rather than broad-spectrum toxicity.
ℹ️ Preliminary study; broader ecological impact and outdoor soil compatibility are not yet certified.

RESPONSIBLE PROTOTYPING & COMPLIANCE

Safety & Material Safety Data Sheets (SDS)

Standard laboratory safety protocols, ingredient handling precautions, and official supplier documentation for supervised academic experimentation.

🧤

Safety

Avoid inhaling fine botanical powders or getting the liquid mixture in your eyes during weighing, mixing, and spray testing.

🌱

Plant patch test

Test on a small area of a single plant leaf and observe for 24–48 hours for signs of leaf damage or discoloration before wider application.

🌤️

Storage

Keep the container tightly closed and clearly labelled. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, heat, food, children, and pets.

📅

Validity

Shelf life is undetermined. The water-based mixture has no validated preservative system; prepare a fresh batch for supervised testing.

DOCUMENTED SUPPLIER PROFILES

Ingredient Safety Overview

Review handling hazards, recommended personal protective equipment (PPE), and official supplier Safety Data Sheets for each formulation component.

01 / BOTANICAL

Neem powder

  • Fine powder can cause dust exposure during weighing and mixing.
  • Avoid inhaling dust and contact with eyes.
  • Wear gloves and safety goggles during preparation.
📄 View Safety Data Sheet (PDF) ↗ Supplier: Praan Naturals
02 / BOTANICAL

Garlic powder or extract

  • Avoid inhaling airborne powder.
  • Dust may irritate the eyes; handle carefully and avoid direct exposure.
  • Use gloves, protective eyewear, and suitable ventilation.
📄 View Safety Data Sheet (PDF) ↗ Supplier: Knowde / Silva
03 / SURFACTANT

Reetha / Soapnut

  • Avoid eye contact and inhalation of fine powder.
  • Soapnut contains saponins, so avoid spraying the mixture toward the face or eyes.
  • Wear gloves and eye protection while preparing the formulation.
📄 View Safety Data Sheet (PDF) ↗ Supplier: Herbalveda
04 / SUPPORTING

Aloe vera powder

  • Avoid breathing airborne powder and prevent eye exposure.
  • Use suitable protective equipment when weighing and mixing.
  • Store in light-resistant, tightly sealed containers.
📄 View Safety Data Sheet (PDF) ↗ Supplier: Spectrum Chemical
05 / SUPPORTING

Glycerine / Glycerol

  • Many supplier grades are not classified as hazardous under GHS, but avoid eye contact.
  • Spills can make floors slippery.
  • Clean spills promptly and consult the SDS for the exact grade used.
📄 View Safety Data Sheet (PDF) ↗ Supplier: Spectrum Chemical

LABORATORY PROTOCOL

Safe Handling Precautions

  • Wear a lab coat, gloves, and safety goggles.
  • Avoid inhaling fine powders and spray mist.
  • Work in a well-ventilated area.
  • Avoid contact with eyes, mouth, and broken skin.
  • Wash hands after preparation.
  • Keep the formulation away from children, pets, food, and drink.
  • Do not ingest the formulation.
  • Do not use it on edible plants intended for consumption until suitability has been assessed.
🩹

EMERGENCY RESPONSE

First Aid

EYES Eyes

Rinse cautiously with clean water for at least 15 minutes. Seek medical attention if irritation persists.

SKIN Skin

Wash thoroughly with soap and water. Seek medical advice if irritation develops or persists.

INHALATION Inhalation

Move to fresh air if dust or mist causes discomfort. Seek medical advice if symptoms continue.

INGESTION Accidental ingestion

Rinse the mouth. Do not induce vomiting; seek medical or poison-control advice.

⚠️ Notice: The exact supplier SDS should be consulted for product-specific first-aid instructions.

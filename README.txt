NaturaSpray Website

Files include index.html, styles.css, script.js, the supplied NaturaSpray prototype photo, and illustrative SVGs.

To run: extract the ZIP, open the folder in VS Code, and open index.html with Live Server (or directly in a browser).

The real prototype photo is used in the hero and gallery.

Actual images included:
- assets/naturaspray-product.jpeg: prototype product photo
- assets/naturaspray-ingredients.jpeg: ingredients and laboratory photo displayed in the Ingredients & purpose section
